About Wood Creations

Information about seller and our custom wood engraving designs.

Personalized Biography

Eloise Ramos has dedicated her life to the art of construction, skillfully maneuvering heavy equipment across the scenic landscapes of Oahu for over three decades. Her expertise in creating enduring foundations for homes, highways, and businesses throughout the island speaks volumes about her commitment and craftsmanship.

Now, as she embraces a new chapter in her life, Eloise channels her creativity and precision into the enchanting world of laser wood art. From the comfort of her blue chair, she transforms wood into exquisite pieces of artwork that used as decoration, serving as both keepsakes and cherished gifts. Each design she creates is of elegance and sophistication, perfect for any space or being worn as a personal statement.
